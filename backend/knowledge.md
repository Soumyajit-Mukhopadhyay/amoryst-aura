# Amoryst Aura Knowledge Base

## Brand Philosophy
Amoryst Aura is a hyper-premium luxury fragrance brand based in India. Our philosophy is "Nature's Canvas," believing that every note has a specific address, soil, season, and harvest. We do not use synthetic fillers; we focus on the purest raw materials sourced globally but heavily rooted in the rich biodiversity of India.

## Key Ingredients
1. **Jasmine Sambac**: Sourced from Tamil Nadu, South India. The Indian jasmine — more indolic and heady. Picked at night when the flowers are fully open. Found in Elysium and Oasis.
2. **Mysore Sandalwood**: Sourced from Karnataka, South India. One of the most precious woody materials in perfumery. Creamy, milky warmth. Found in Twilight, Oasis, and Mirage.
3. **Lotus Absolute**: Sourced from Rajasthan, Western India. Extracted from lotus flowers grown in Rajasthani lakes. Aquatic but warm. Found in Oasis.
4. **Kashmiri Saffron**: Sourced from Pampore, Kashmir, Northern India. Harvested by hand in October, one filament at a time. The most expensive spice in the world. Found in Reserve: Saffron Dusk.
5. **Cardamom**: Sourced from Kerala, Southwest India. Warm-spiced and slightly camphoraceous. Kerala cardamom is uniquely bright. Found in Horizon and Reserve: Saffron Dusk.

## About Amoryst Aura
Amoryst Aura was born from the belief that India's botanical heritage deserves a place at the pinnacle of global luxury perfumery. Every fragrance is hand-composed by our in-house perfumers who blend time-honored Indian ingredients with modern olfactory artistry. Our bottles are designed by artisan glassmakers in Firozabad, India, and each cap is hand-finished with 24K gold leaf.
